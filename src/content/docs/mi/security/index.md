I hangaia a Onetime Secret hei kawe i ngā kōrero matatapu mai i tētahi tangata
ki tētahi atu, kia kore ai e waiho noa ki roto i ngā pouakaīmēra, i ngā rangitaki
kōrerorero, i ngā pūnaha tīkiti rānei. Ka whakamārama tēnei wāhanga i te āhua i
hangaia ai te ratonga kia haumaru ai taua mahi, me te wāhi e kitea ai ngā
taipitopito.

## Te Tauira Haumaru — He Whakarāpopoto

- **Urunga kotahi anake.** Kua hoahoatia te karere huna kia kotahi anake te
  tirohanga, kātahi ka mukua mō ake tonu. Kia oti te pānui (kia pau rānei), kua
  ngaro.
- **Te whakamuhumuhu i te whakawhitinga me te noho.** Ka whakamuhumuhua ngā
  karere huna i te whakawhitinga me te noho, i ngā mahere katoa.
- **Te tiaki kupu karapa.** Ka taea e koe te here kia whakaurua he kupu karapa
  hei tiro i te karere huna — he papa haumaru tāpiri e kore e taea te huaki mā
  te hononga anake.
- **Kua hoahoatia kia whāiti te wā.** He wā paunga tō ia karere huna; kōwhiria
  te wā ora poto rawa e tika ana kia iti ai te mōrearea.
- **Te whakawareware i mua i te pānui.** Mēnā kāore anō te karere huna kia
  tirohia, ka taea e koe te whakawareware kia kore rawa e pānuitia.
- **Te whakaiti raraunga.** E whai ana mātou ki te kohi me te pupuri i te mea e
  hiahiatia ana anake — tirohia [Whakaiti Raraunga](/mi/principles/data-minimization).

## Tūhuratia Tēnei Wāhanga

- **[Tiaki Raraunga](/mi/security/data-protection)** — ngā mea e penapenatia ana e mātou, te roa, te wāhi e noho ai, me te hāngai o ēnei ki ō hiahiatanga ūnga.
- **[Ngā Tikanga Haumaru Pai](/mi/security-best-practices)** — he aratohu whaihua mō te tohatoha haumaru i ngā karere huna, tae atu ki ngā painga o ngā Rohe Ritenga.
- **[Whākinga Whakaraeraetanga](/mi/security/vulnerability-disclosure)** — te huarahi hei pūrongo haepapa i tētahi raru haumaru.

## Ngā Hononga Hāngai

- **[Ō Mātou Mātāpono](/mi/principles)** — Tūmataitinga i te Tuatahi, Whakawhitiwhiti Kōrero, me te Whakaiti Raraunga.
- **[Ngā Rohe Pūtoi Raraunga](/mi/regions)** — kōwhiria te wāhi e tukatukahia ai, e penapenatia ai hoki ō raraunga.
- **[Manaaki-ā-Kiri](https://github.com/onetimesecret/onetimesecret)** — whakahaerehia a Onetime Secret i runga i tō ake hanganga kia riro katoa i a koe te mana whakahaere.
