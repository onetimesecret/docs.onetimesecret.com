Ka whakamārama tēnei whārangi i te āhua o tā Onetime Secret whakahaere i ō
raraunga: ngā mea e penapenatia ana, te roa, te wāhi e noho ai, me te āhua e
tautoko ai tērā i tō ake hōtaka ūnga.

## Ngā Mea e Penapenatia ana e Mātou, me te Roa

- **Ko te ihirangi karere huna** ka whakamuhumuhua, ā, mō te tikinga kotahi
  anake. Kia tirohia te karere huna — kia tae rānei ki tōna wā paunga — ka
  mukua mō ake tonu.
- **Kua hangaia te wā paunga ki roto.** He wā ora tō ia karere huna (ka taea
  te whirihora i roto i ngā tepe o tō mahere); kāore he mea i whakaarohia kia
  noho tonu mō ake tonu.
- **He raraunga meta iti noa.** E ai ki tō mātou mātāpono [Whakaiti Raraunga](/mi/principles/data-minimization),
  e whai ana mātou ki te pupuri i ngā raraunga meta e hiahiatia ana anake hei
  whakahaere i te ratonga.

## Te Whakamuhumuhu

Ka **whakamuhumuhua ngā karere huna i te whakawhitinga me te noho** i ia
mahere. Ka tiakina te whakawhitinga ki te TLS, ā, mō ngā rohe ritenga ka
whakahaere aunoatia e mātou te tuku me te whakahou i ngā tiwhikete SSL/TLS.

Mō ngā kōrero tino matatapu, ka taea e koe te tāpiri i tētahi papa tiaki anō mā
te whakahohe i tētahi **kupu karapa**, mā te wehe i ngā kōrero ki ngā karere
huna maha, mā te kōwhiri hoki i te wā paunga poto rawa e tika ana — tirohia
[Ngā Tikanga Haumaru Pai](/mi/security-best-practices).

## Te Wāhi e Tukatukahia ai ō Raraunga (te Nohonga)

Ka taea e koe te kōwhiri i te rohe e tukatukahia ai, e penapenatia ai hoki ō
raraunga — i tēnei wā ko te EU, te UK, te US, te CA, me te NZ. Mā konei ka noho
tata ō raraunga ki ō kaiwhakamahi, ki roto hoki i tētahi mana ture e hāngai ana
ki ō hiahiatanga. Tirohia
[Ngā Rohe Pūtoi Raraunga](/mi/regions) mō ngā taipitopito me ngā pito mutunga.

## Te Ūnga

Kua hoahoatia a Onetime Secret hei tautoko i ō mahi ūnga; e kore e whakakapi i
ō ake whakahaere, kaupapahere, arotake ture hoki.

- **GDPR / te tiaki raraunga.** Ko te nohonga raraunga ā-rohe, ko ngā raraunga
  wā-poto, me te whakaiti raraunga i hoahoatia hei āwhina i a koe ki te
  whakatutuki i ō herenga tiaki raraunga. I te nuinga o ngā whakaturanga, ko
  koe te kaiwhakahaere raraunga (data controller), ko Onetime Secret te
  kaitukatuka (data processor) mō ngā raraunga iti e pā ana.
- **HIPAA.** Pērā i te kōrero i ā mātou [keehi whakamahi](/mi/custom-domains/use-cases),
  ka taea e Onetime Secret te whakarato i tētahi ara haumaru ake i te īmēra mō
  te tuku i ngā pūnga whaimana urunga tuatahi, engari me whakamahi hei mea
  taupua, kaua hei pūnaha pupuri tūturu mō ngā raraunga PHI. Honoa ki tētahi
  pūnaha ūnga motuhake mō ngā mahi PHI haere tonu.
- **Ngā tiwhikete, ngā DPA, me ngā anga motuhake.** Mō ngā pātai mō ngā
  tiwhikete, mō tētahi Whakaaetanga Tukatuka Raraunga (DPA), mō tētahi anga
  ture motuhake rānei, whakapā atu ki **support@onetimesecret.com**.

Mō ngā whakahaere he pakari ngā hiahiatanga whakahaere raraunga, mā te [manaaki-ā-kiri](https://github.com/onetimesecret/onetimesecret)
ka noho katoa ngā mea ki roto i tō ake hanganga.
