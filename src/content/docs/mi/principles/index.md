E tū ana a Onetime Secret kia taea ai e koe te tohatoha kōrero matatapu me te kore e waiho kia takoto noa. Ko ēnei mātāpono e whakaahua ana i ia whakatau papatono, whakahaere hoki e mahia ana e mātou — ā, i te mea kua tuhia, ka taea e koe te pupuri i a mātou kia ū ki ēnei.

## Ō Mātou Here

- **[Tūmataitinga i te Tuatahi](/mi/principles/privacy-first)** — ngā tikanga aroturuki me te hokohoko e karohia whakaaro nui ana e mātou, me te āhua o tā mātou pupuri i aua here kia taea te manatoko
- **[Whakawhitiwhiti Kōrero](/mi/principles/communication)** — te take e tuku iti rawa ai mātou i te īmēra, me ngā momo whāiti e toru e here ai mātou i a mātou anō
- **[Whakaiti Raraunga](/mi/principles/data-minimization)** — te iti o ngā mea e penapenatia ana e mātou, te roa e puritia ai, me ngā mea e kore rawa e kohia e mātou

## He Aha Te Tikanga o ngā Mātāpono

Mā ngā mātāpono kua tuhia mātou e pupuri kia pūmau. Ka whakatauria e ēnei ngā whakatau papatono i mua i tō rātou huringa hei tautohetohe, ā, ka hoatu ki a koe he tuhinga tūmatanui, pūmau hoki hei ine i a mātou. Ina tō te tupu me te tūmataiti ki ngā aronga rerekē, kua kī kē ēnei whārangi ko tēhea ka toa.

Mō te taha hangarau o ēnei here — te whakamuhumuhu, te whakahaere raraunga, me te pūrongo whakaraeraetanga — tirohia [Haumarutanga me te Whakapono](/mi/security).

**He pātai mō tō mātou huarahi?** [Whakapā mai](https://onetimesecret.com/feedback).
