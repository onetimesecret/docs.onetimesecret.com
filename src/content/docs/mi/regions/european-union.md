## Te Taiao Ture

Ka whakahaerehia te rohe EU i raro i te **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, tētahi o ngā anga tiaki raraunga whānui rawa atu o te ao. Ko Tiamana, te whenua e whakahaerehia ana te pūtoi raraunga, e whakamahi ana hoki i te **Bundesdatenschutzgesetz (BDSG)**, e tāpiri ana ki te GDPR.

### Mō te Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga

Ko tēnei rohe e whakahaerehia ana e <a href="https://www.hetzner.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Hetzner</a>, he kaiwhakarato pūtoi raraunga nō Tiamana, ko tōna tūranga kei Gunzenhausen, e mahi ana i raro i te mana whakahaere o te EU. Ka tukuna e Hetzner ngā rongoā kapua whai hua, arā tōna aronga ki te tūmataiti me te tiaki raraunga i raro i te GDPR. Kāore ngā mōhiohio whaiaro a ngā kiritaki tūmataiti e whakaaturia i roto i ngā rekoata WHOIS tūmatanui, ā, ka mahi te kamupene i raro i ngā ture tiaki raraunga taumaha a Tiamana me te EU.

### Ngā Āhuatanga Ture Matua

- Ka whakarato te GDPR i ngā mana kaha ki ngā tāngata mō ō rātou raraunga, tae atu ki te uru atu, te whakatika, te muku, me te kawenga
- Me whai take ā-ture te tukatuka raraunga (hei tauira, te whakaaetanga, te pānga tika, te whakaritenga kirimana rānei)
- Ko te Kaikomihana ā-Motu o Tiamana mō te Tiaki Raraunga me te Mōhiohio Kore-Ārai (BfDI) te mana tirotiro
- He ture pakeke mō te whakawhitinga raraunga ki tāwāhi, ki waho atu o te EU/EEA

## Ā Hea Whakaarohia Tēnei Rohe

- Kei te EU, EEA rānei tō whakahaere, tō hunga kaiwhakamahi rānei e noho matua ana
- Me ū koe ki ngā whakahau o te GDPR
- E hiahia ana koe kia noho tō raraunga i roto i te European Union
- Kei a koe he kiritaki e tūmanako ana ki ngā paerewa tiaki raraunga taumata-EU
