## Hanganga

- **Wāhi**: Hillsboro, Oregon, United States
- **URL**: [us.onetimesecret.com](https://us.onetimesecret.com)
- **Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga**: <a href="https://www.hetzner.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Hetzner</a>
- **CNAME Rohe Ritenga**: `identity.us.onetime.co`

## Te Taiao Ture

Kāore he ture ā-kāwanatanga kotahi, whānui hoki, mō te tiaki raraunga i United States. Engari, ka whakahaerehia te tiaki raraunga mā te huinga o ngā ture ā-kāwanatanga me ngā ture ā-kāwana-ā-rohe e pā ana ki ētahi rāngai, momo raraunga rānei.

### Mō te Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga

Ko tēnei rohe e whakahaerehia ana e <a href="https://www.hetzner.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Hetzner</a>, he kaiwhakarato pūtoi raraunga nō Tiamana, ko tōna tūranga kei Gunzenhausen, e mahi ana i raro i te mana whakahaere o te EU. Ahakoa kei United States ōna pūtoi raraunga, ka pupuri tonu a Hetzner i tōna aronga tūmataiti e ū ana ki ngā paerewa tiaki raraunga a Tiamana me te EU. Kāore ngā mōhiohio whaiaro a ngā kiritaki tūmataiti e whakaaturia i roto i ngā rekoata WHOIS tūmatanui.

### Ngā Āhuatanga Ture Matua

- Ka whai take ngā ture ā-kāwanatanga pēnei i te HIPAA (raraunga hauora), te GLBA (raraunga pūtea), me te COPPA (raraunga tamariki) ki ētahi rāngai
- Kei te piki haere te whai take o ngā ture tūmataiti ā-kāwana-ā-rohe, tae atu ki te **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)** me tāna whakarerekētanga, te **California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)**
- He ture tiaki raraunga whānui anō ō ētahi atu kāwana-ā-rohe, tae atu ki Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, me Utah
- Ko Oregon, te wāhi e noho ana tēnei pūtoi raraunga, i whakamana i te **Oregon Consumer Privacy Act**, kua whai mana mai i Hūrae 2024

## Ā Hea Whakaarohia Tēnei Rohe

- Kei United States tō whakahaere, tō hunga kaiwhakamahi rānei e noho matua ana
- Me ū koe ki ngā ture tiaki raraunga ā-kāwanatanga, ā-kāwana-ā-rohe rānei o United States
- E hiahia ana koe kia noho tō raraunga i roto i United States
- Kei a koe he kiritaki kei Amerika ki te Raki, ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te urunga tere iti mai i te uru o United States
