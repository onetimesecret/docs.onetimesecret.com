## Hanganga

- **Wāhi**: London, United Kingdom
- **URL**: [uk.onetimesecret.com](https://uk.onetimesecret.com)
- **Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga**: <a href="https://upcloud.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">UpCloud</a> (Helsinki, Finland)
- **CNAME Rohe Ritenga**: `identity.ingress.onetime.co` (anycast)

## DNS Rohe Ritenga

Hei whakatau i tētahi rohe ritenga ki tēnei rohe, waihangatia he rēkōta CNAME:

| Momo Rēkōta | Kaiwhakataki           | Uara                           |
| ----------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| CNAME       | `secrets.example.com`  | `identity.ingress.onetime.co`  |

Kia mōhio, ka whakamahi te rohe UK i tētahi CNAME anycast, kaua i tētahi rohe-iti ā-rohe motuhake.

Tirohia te [Aratohu Whakatū Rohe Ritenga](/mi/custom-domains/setup-guide) mō ngā tohutohu katoa.

## Te Taiao Ture

Ko te anga tiaki raraunga o United Kingdom e whakahaerehia ana e te **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** me te **Data Protection Act 2018**. I muri i a Brexit, e pupuri tonu ana a United Kingdom i tāna ake anga tiaki raraunga, e tino rite ana ki te EU GDPR.

### Mō te Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga

Ko tēnei rohe e whakahaerehia ana e <a href="https://upcloud.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">UpCloud</a>, he kaiwhakarato hanganga kapua nō Ōropi, i whakaturia i te tau 2011, ko tōna tari matua kei Helsinki, Finland. I te mea he kaiwhakarato mana motuhake nō Ōropi, ka penapenahia ngā raraunga katoa e pā ana ki ngā pūkete ki Finland anake, i raro i ngā ture tiaki raraunga a Finland me te EU. Ka whakahaerehia e UpCloud ngā pūtoi raraunga puta noa i ngā wāhi maha o Ōropi, tae atu ki London, te wāhi e whakahaerehia ana tēnei rohe.

### Ngā Āhuatanga Ture Matua

- Ko Te Tari o te Kaikomihana Mōhiohio (Information Commissioner's Office, ICO) te mana tirotiro motuhake
- Ka pupuri te UK GDPR i ngā mātāpono me ngā mana matua o te EU GDPR, tae atu ki ngā mana o te tangata mō ōna raraunga me ngā whakahau take ā-ture
- He whakatau taurite tā United Kingdom nā te Kōmihana Ōropi, e taea ai te rere kore-ārai o ngā raraunga mai i te EU/EEA
- Ka tāpiri te Data Protection Act 2018 ki te UK GDPR, ā, he wāhanga anō ōna e pā ana ki ngā ratonga whakahau ture me ngā ratonga mōhiohio a United Kingdom
