## Te Taiao Ture

Ko te anga tiaki raraunga o Aotearoa e whakahaerehia ana e te **Privacy Act 2020**, nāna i whakakapi te Privacy Act 1993. Ka whakatakoto te Ture i ngā mātāpono tiaki mōhiohio tūmataiti kotahi tekau mā toru, e pā ana ki ngā tari e whakahaere ana i ngā mōhiohio whaiaro.

### Mō te Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga

Ko tēnei rohe e whakahaerehia ana e <a href="https://catalystcloud.nz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Catalyst Cloud</a>, he kaiwhakarato kapua nō Aotearoa, nāna anō i hanga, ā, e whakahaerehia ana e Aotearoa, e tautoko ana i te ōhanga matihiko o te motu. Ka penapenahia ngā raraunga kiritaki katoa ki uta, puta noa i ngā pūtoi raraunga uta e toru, ā, ka noho tonu ngā raraunga ki Aotearoa, e tiakina ana e ngā ture me ngā ture tūmataiti ā-rohe. He kaiwhakarato whakaaetia a Catalyst Cloud mō ngā tari a Te Kāwanatanga o Aotearoa, ā, kei a rātou ngā tohu ISO mō te whakahaere haumarutanga mōhiohio.

### Ngā Āhuatanga Ture Matua

- Kei roto i te Privacy Act 2020 ngā whakahau pūrongo pakaru raraunga
- Ko Te Tari o te Kaikomihana Tūmataiti te kaitirotiro i te ū ki te ture, ā, ka torotoro hoki i ngā amuamu
- He whakatau taurite tā Aotearoa nā te Kōmihana Ōropi, e āwhina ana i te whakawhitinga raraunga ki waenga i a Aotearoa me te EU
- Ka whai take te Ture ki ngā tari o tāwāhi e mahi arumoni ana i Aotearoa

## Ā Hea Whakaarohia Tēnei Rohe

- Kei Aotearoa, kei te rohe o te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa rānei tō whakahaere, tō hunga kaiwhakamahi rānei e noho matua ana
- Me ū koe ki te Privacy Act 2020 o Aotearoa
- E hiahia ana koe kia noho tō raraunga i roto i Aotearoa
- Kei a koe he kiritaki kei Oceania, ā, e hiahia ana koe ki tētahi tūnga ā-rohe
