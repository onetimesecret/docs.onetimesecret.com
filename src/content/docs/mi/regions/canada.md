## Te Taiao Ture

Ko te ture tūmataiti ā-motu o Canada, arā ko te **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)**, e whakahaere ana i te kohikohi, te whakamahinga, me te tuku i ngā mōhiohio whaiaro i roto i ngā mahi arumoni. He maha ngā porowini e pupuri ana i ō rātou ake ture tūmataiti ka whai take anō pea.

### Mō te Kaiwhakarato Pūtoi Raraunga

Ko tēnei rohe e whakahaerehia ana e <a href="https://www.digitalocean.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">DigitalOcean</a>, he kaiwhakarato kapua nō Amerika, ko tōna tari matua kei Broomfield, Colorado, e tuku ratonga ana ki ngā miriona kaiwhakawaehere puta noa i te ao. E ū ana a DigitalOcean ki te GDPR mō āna kiritaki Ōropi, e tautoko ana i te kawenga raraunga, ā, e whakaputa ana i ngā pūrongo mārama e whakaatu ana i ngā tono raraunga a te kāwanatanga. E whakatinanahia ana e te kamupene ngā mana whakahaere haumaru kaha, ā, ka whakaputaina ngā pūrongo arotake.

### Ngā Āhuatanga Ture Matua

- E hiahiatia ana e PIPEDA te whakaaetanga tino mārama mō te kohikohi me te whakamahinga raraunga
- Ko Te Tari o te Kaikomihana Tūmataiti o Canada (Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada) te kaitirotiro i te ū ki te ture
- He whakatau taurite tā Canada nā te Kōmihana Ōropi, e āwhina ana i te whakawhitinga raraunga ki waenga i a Canada me te EU
- Ka tāpiri whakahau anō pea ngā ture ā-porowini (hei tauira, ko te PIPA o Alberta, te Ture 25 o Quebec)

## Ā Hea Whakaarohia Tēnei Rohe

- Kei Canada tō whakahaere, tō hunga kaiwhakamahi rānei e noho matua ana
- Me ū koe ki te PIPEDA, ki tētahi ture tūmataiti ā-porowini rānei
- E hiahia ana koe kia noho tō raraunga i roto i ngā rohe o Canada
- Kei Amerika ki te Raki ō kiritaki, ā, e hiahia ana koe ki tētahi kōwhiringa kei waenganui pū o te rohe matawhenua
