Ka whakarato a Onetime Secret i ngā rohe pūtoi raraunga e rima: Canada (CA), European Union (EU), Aotearoa (NZ), United Kingdom (UK), me United States (US). Ka āwhina tēnei aratohu i a koe ki te mārama i te tikanga o te kōwhiringa rohe me te āhua o te kōwhiri i te tika mō ō hiahiatanga.

## He Aha Te Tikanga o te Kōwhiringa Rohe

He nui te kōwhiri i te rohe pūtoi raraunga tika mō ētahi take:

1. **Rangatiratanga Raraunga**: He rerekē ngā ture tiaki raraunga me ngā ture o ngā rohe rerekē.
2. **Takamuatanga**: Ko te kōwhiri i tētahi rohe tata atu ki tō pānga kaiwhakamahi matua ka taea te whakaitihia i te takamuatanga.
3. **Ūnga**: He whakahau motuhake ō ētahi whakahaere mō te wāhi ka taea te penapena i ō rātou raraunga.

## Hoahoa Kore-Tohatoha

Ka whakamahi a Onetime Secret i tētahi hoahoa kore-tohatoha, e whakaū ana i te motuhake raraunga katoa i waenganui i ngā rohe:

- **Ngā Pūkete Motuhake**: Ko te waihanga pūkete i tētahi rohe e tino motuhake ana i ngā pūkete i ngā rohe kē, ahakoa ka whakamahi koe i te wāhitau īmēra kotahi.
- **Kāore he Mahi Whakawhiti-Pūtoi**: Kāore e taea e koe te mahi (pēnei i te whakawareware karere huna) i waenganui i ngā pūtoi raraunga. Ka pupuri ia pūtoi i ōna ake karere huna me ngā raraunga kaiwhakamahi.
- **Te Utu Pūmau mō ngā Kaiwhakamahi Utu**: Mō ngā pūkete utu, ahakoa kāore he raraunga kaiwhakamahi e tohatohahia i waenganui i ngā pūtoi, ka mōhiotia tō tūnga ohaurunga puta noa i ngā rohe mā tā mātou kaiwhakarato utu, Stripe.

## Pēhea te Kōwhiri i Tō Rohe

Whakaarohia ēnei āhuatanga i te wā e kōwhiri ana i tō rohe pūtoi raraunga:

### Kāore he Pūkete

- Ka taea te tuku i ngā tono ki onetimesecret.com ki tētahi pūtoi raraunga hohe.
- Ka taea e koe te kōwhiri i tētahi rohe motuhake mā te whakatere tōtika ki tētahi rohe (hei tauira, [ca.onetimesecret.com](https://ca.onetimesecret.com/)).
- Ka mārama tonu mai te rohe i te hononga kua waihangahia (hei tauira, `us.onetimesecret.com/secret/abcd1234`).

### He Pūkete

- I te wā e waihanga ana i tētahi pūkete, ka kōwhiri koe i tētahi rohe pūtoi raraunga. Ka whai wāhi ngā mahere katoa — koreutu me te utu — ki ngā rohe katoa.
- Ka takiuru koe ki te rohe kotahi i rēhita ai koe (hei tauira, mēnā i rēhita koe ki `eu.onetimesecret.com`, ko reira tō wāhi takiuru).

### Ngā Whakaarohanga Tāpiri

1. **Mō ngā Tangata Takitahi**:
   - Te hiahia whaiaro
   - Te tata ki tō wāhi mō te urunga tere ake pea
   - Ngā āwangawanga rangatiratanga raraunga whaiaro

2. **Mō ngā Pakihi**:
   - Ngā whakahau ā-ture me te here
   - Te wāhi o tō pānga kiritaki matua
   - Ngā hiahiatanga ūnga ā-ahumahi

3. **Ngā Whakaarohanga Hangarau**:
   - Ngā whakahau takamuatanga mō tō taupānga
   - Te whakaurunga ki ētahi atu ratonga, pūnaha rānei

## Ngā Pātai Auau

**P: Ka taea e au te huri i taku rohe i muri i te whakatū i taku pūkete?**
W: Āe. Tirohia [Te Huri i Tō Rohe](/mi/regions/switching-regions) mō ngā tohutohu mā te hipa e kapi ana i ngā pūkete koreutu, ngā ohaurunga utu, me te nekehanga rohe ritenga.

**P: Ka pā taku kōwhiringa rohe ki te haumaru o aku karere huna?**
W: Kāo, ka whakarato ngā rohe katoa i te taumata haumaru teitei kotahi. Ko te kōwhiringa matua e pā ana ki te noho raraunga me te takamuatanga pea.

**P: He rerekē ngā utu i waenganui i ngā rohe?**
W: He motuhake ngā utu mō ia rohe — ka taea e koe te utu ki tō moni ā-rohe, ā, mā Stripe e whakahaere aunoatia te whakawhiti moni. Kei roto i ngā mahere Identity Plus ngā rohe ritenga mutunga-kore puta noa i ngā pūtoi raraunga katoa i raro i te ohaurunga kotahi. Tirohia tō mātou [whārangi utu](https://onetimesecret.com/pricing) mō ngā kōrero hou rawa.
