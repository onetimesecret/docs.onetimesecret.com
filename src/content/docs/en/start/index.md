Onetime Secret lets you share sensitive information through a link. The
recipient reveals the secret once, then it is deleted.
This documentation covers creating, sharing and managing those self-destructing
messages — on the hosted service at
[onetimesecret.com](https://onetimesecret.com/), on an instance you run
yourself, or through the API.

Ready to try it? [Send your first secret](/en/start/send-your-first-secret/)
without creating an account.
