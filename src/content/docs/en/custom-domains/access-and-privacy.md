Three per-domain settings decide what your custom domain exposes and who can get
an account through it: **Privacy Options**, **Signin Settings**, and **Signup
Settings**. Each is configured on your Domain Dashboard, and each is
domain-specific.

:::note
This page describes what each setting does and what it changes for visitors.
The controls themselves live on your Domain Dashboard.
:::

## Privacy Options


Enhanced privacy features are part of the **Identity Plus** plan. **Privacy
Options** are the per-domain controls that let you decide how much your
[custom domain](/en/custom-domains) exposes to visitors and how it behaves when
people interact with it.

Depending on your configuration, these controls can include:

- Whether your [branded homepage](/en/custom-domains/homepage-and-incoming#homepage-secrets) is public or disabled.
- Whether visitors may create secrets, or only open links you generate.
- How recipients and senders experience your domain.

The goal is to let your custom domain match your organization's privacy posture,
rather than exposing more than you intend.

## Signin Settings


Signin Settings control how existing users authenticate when they access your
custom domain. Where the Signup Settings described below govern account
_creation_, Signin Settings govern account _access_ — the login experience your
users see on your branded domain.

Typical uses include:

- Choosing which sign-in methods are available on your domain.
- Requiring [Single Sign-On (SSO)](/en/organizations/sso) so users authenticate through your identity provider.
- Aligning the sign-in experience with your organization's security requirements.

## Signup Settings


Signup Settings control whether — and how — new users can create an account
through your custom domain. They let you decide who is allowed to onboard from
your branded experience, rather than leaving signup open to anyone who finds the
page.

Typical uses include:

- Turning signup on or off for your domain.
- Limiting who can sign up (for example, restricting to particular email domains).
- Aligning the signup experience with your organization's access policies.

## Related features

- [Homepage and Incoming Secrets](/en/custom-domains/homepage-and-incoming) — turn public secret creation on or off, and collect secrets from others through your domain.
- [Single Sign-On (SSO)](/en/organizations/sso) — let people sign in with their existing corporate credentials.
