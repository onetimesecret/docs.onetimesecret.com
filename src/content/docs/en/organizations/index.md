An organization is the shared workspace that owns resources several people use
together, rather than each person working from an isolated account. Members and
administrators manage those shared resources in one place.

An organization holds:

- Shared [custom domains](/en/custom-domains) and their settings.
- Organization membership and roles — see [Inviting members](/en/organizations/inviting-members).
- Authentication for everyone in it — see [Single Sign-On (SSO)](/en/organizations/sso).
- A common record of what happened to the organization's secrets — see
  [Secret Activity](/en/organizations/audit-trail).

## You already have one

Every signed-in account belongs to an organization. If you never invite anyone,
it stays in the background: it is created for you the first time you reach a
feature that needs it, and it cannot be deleted.

The app uses **organization** and **workspace** for the same thing, and you will
see both words on screen.

## Roles

An organization has three roles, and each one includes everything the role below
it can do:

- **Owner** — full access to all organization settings and members.
- **Admin** — can manage members and most organization settings.
- **Member** — basic access to organization resources.

Organization settings are reachable only by owners and admins. A member uses the
organization's shared resources but does not administer them.

:::note
This is separate from your own account settings. Roles here govern the shared
organization, not your personal sign-in.
:::
